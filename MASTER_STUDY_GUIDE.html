1. Dataset Composition & Split Strategy

Images were collected across 6 distinct acquisition batches (40x-001 through 006) by two independent annotators on distinct, non-overlapping tissue fields. To guarantee zero data leakage, a strict Stratified Patient-Level Split was established:

Dataset Scaling to ~2,500 Tiles / Class (~10,000 Tiles Total)

In Models 4 and 5, the training set was scaled from 2,128 to 10,000 tiles using targeted geometric (random rotation, elastic distortion) and photometric (hue/saturation jitter, noise) augmentations applied heavily to dispersive and noncohesive. Validation and locked test sets remained 100% unaugmented.

4. Phenotype Failure Analysis: High Performers vs. Low Performers

High Performing Classes (>88% F1)

  • cohesive (90.4% F1): Huge solid sheets of malignant cells. Very dense, uniform circularity. ViT CLS tokens detect solid glandular patterns with 95.5% recall.
  • no_feature (88.2% F1): Fibrous acellular stroma, keratin pearls, necrosis. Low nuclei count and high pink stroma fraction make it clean to separate.
  • dispersive (84.9% F1): Solitary detached cells. In Model 5, the recurrent loop achieved 100% precision (zero false alarms).

The Challenging Class: noncohesive (65–69% F1)

  • The Intermediate Cluster Dilemma: Noncohesive cells travel in small detached groups (15 to 50 cells).
  • Why Spatial Pooling Fails It: All models so far used mean() and max() pooling over the 16 crops. This averages out the empty space between clusters, making 15-cell clusters look like a large solid cohesive sheet!
  • The Test Set Confusion: Out of 29 noncohesive test tiles, 12 were mistakenly predicted as cohesive.

5. Architectural Blueprint for Model 6: MS-XNet

To surpass Model 4's 83.2% F1 and resolve the noncohesive bottleneck while keeping Model 5's 100% dispersive precision:

1. Cross-Attention Pooling
Replaces mean/max pooling with Multi-Head Attention (H=8). Uses TileCNN global view as Query and Phikon crops as Keys/Values to explicitly measure cluster distances.
2. Model 5 Recurrent Feedback Gate
Preserves the Sigmoid recurrent loop that guaranteed 100% precision on solitary dispersive cells.
3. Morphological Injection
Injects mean_nn_distance and nuclei_density into the final head, giving direct physical cluster separation signal.
